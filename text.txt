    step 1: predict: the model uses its current weight and bias to predict the outputs.
    step 2: measure error: MSELoss compares its predictions with [3, 5, 7, 9].
    step 3: calculate gradients: loss.backward() calculates how changes in the parameters would affect the loss.
    step 4: update parameters: optimize.step() adjusts the weight and bias in directions intended to reduce the loss.
    * Notice something important: the training data doesn't change, but the model's parameters do. That's why its predictions improve over time.
